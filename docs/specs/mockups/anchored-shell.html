Nexui shell, version 2

A clickable sketch of the anchored shell with the new look. The data is made up. It's Saturday 26 September, 10:40.

Try

  • On Home, tap Move to today or Not now on a suggestion. The zone keeps its size.
  • Tap + and type lunch with Sam fri at 1 at Lilia. Each field gets a label, and marked values came from your words.
  • Type something vague, like groceries. Nexui asks you to check it instead of saving.
  • In Calendar, look at the free time between events, then pick another day and watch the summary line.
  • Tick a task. It checks, folds away, and Undo brings it back.

What changed from version 1

  • Violet-tinted paper with white cards, and yellow as the accent for main buttons and the active tab.
  • Highlights are a marker stroke with a text label next to them.
  • The draft card explains itself: labeled rows and one "why" line.
  • Events are blocks sized by length. Tasks are plain lines. Notes are cards.
  • Home has Today, Nexui suggests, Recent, and a hint that opens +.
